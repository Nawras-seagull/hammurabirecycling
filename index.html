Our Process

Four Stages, One Circular System

Every tire that enters our facility follows the same closed-loop path — from collection on Iraq's roads to a finished, market-ready material.

01

Collection & Sorting

We carefully collect used tires from various sources across Iraq, ensuring proper and accurate segregation.

02

Shredding

Tires are fed into powerful shredders, breaking them down into smaller pieces ready for the next step, which is grinding.

03

Grinding & Granulation

These shredded pieces are further processed into granules of various sizes, with steel and fabric removed.

04

Product Creation

The resulting rubber granules and powder are then used to create a diverse range of valuable products.
