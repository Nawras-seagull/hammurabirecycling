Our Products

Premium Raw Materials, Made in Iraq

At Hammurabi Golden Gate, we believe quality is the result of intelligent effort. Our advanced recycling lines process end-of-life tires into clean, uniform, and high-value materials.

By choosing our products, you are supporting Iraqi industry and ensuring a sustainable supply chain for your business.

Rubber Granules (Crumb Rubber)

High-purity rubber for construction, sports, and manufacturing.

Our flagship product. We produce steel-free and fiber-free rubber granules in various mesh sizes to meet specific industrial requirements. Uniform in size and free of contaminants, our granules are the perfect alternative to virgin rubber.

Sports SurfacesEssential infill for artificial turf football fields and running tracks.
Playground SafetyUsed in manufacturing impact-absorbing rubber tiles to keep children safe.
Asphalt ModificationMixed with bitumen to create "Rubberized Asphalt," which creates roads that are quieter, more durable, and resistant to cracking under the hot Iraqi sun.
Molded ProductsUsed to create mats, bumpers, and industrial flooring.

Rubber Mulch

The superior choice for landscaping and playgrounds.

Our rubber mulch mimics the look of natural wood bark but offers superior durability and safety. It does not rot, absorb water, or attract insects (like termites).

LandscapingGarden beds and decorative areas (requires zero maintenance).
PlaygroundsLoose-fill surface that provides excellent shock absorption for falls.
Equestrian ArenasProvides a soft, dust-free footing for horses.

Tire Derived Fuel (TDF)

A high-energy, cost-effective alternative fuel source.

Tire Derived Fuel (TDF) is composed of shredded tires chipped into specific sizes (usually 2 to 4 inches). It is a superior energy source for energy-intensive industries. TDF produces higher heat output than coal and wood, making it an excellent choice for reducing fuel costs.

Ideal For

Cement KilnsTDF is widely used in the cement industry as a coal substitute.
Power PlantsFor waste-to-energy power generation.
Paper & Pulp MillsFor industrial boilers.

Key Benefits

  • Higher calorific value than coal.
  • Lower moisture content.
  • Reliable, consistent supply.

Reclaimed Steel Wire

High-quality scrap metal for the steel industry.

Tires contain high-tensile steel wire for reinforcement. During our recycling process, we magnetically separate this steel, cleaning it of rubber residue. This results in high-grade scrap steel ready for smelting and repurposing.

Applications

Steel Mills & FoundriesReclaimed wire feeds directly back into steel production.
Metal RecyclingUsed in smelting and repurposing operations.
