Our Story

Turning Waste into Opportunity for a Cleaner Iraq

Who We Are

At Hammurabi Golden Gate, we are more than just a recycling facility; we are an environmental solution provider and a driver of industrial growth. Located in the heart of Iraq, we have established a state-of-the-art facility dedicated to the responsible collection and processing of end-of-life tires.

We bridge the gap between waste management and manufacturing. By utilizing advanced shredding and granulation technology, we transform hazardous tire waste into valuable raw materials—such as rubber granules, steel, and Tire Derived Fuel (TDF)—serving industries ranging from construction to energy.

Why We Exist

The Challenge

For decades, millions of used tires have accumulated in landfills and illegal dump sites across Iraq. These stockpiles pose severe risks:

Fire Hazards

Uncontrollable fires that release toxic black smoke.

Health Risks

Breeding grounds for disease-carrying pests.

Land Waste

Occupying valuable land that could be used for development.

Hammurabi Golden Gate was founded to stop this cycle. We view every scrap tire not as trash, but as a resource waiting to be harvested.

Our Mission

To safeguard Iraq's environment

To safeguard Iraq's environment by eliminating tire waste through sustainable recycling, while bolstering the national economy by producing high-quality, locally-manufactured resources.

Our Vision

The regional benchmark

To become the leading benchmark for sustainable tire waste management in the Middle East, ensuring that no tire in Iraq ends up in a landfill, burns in the open, or pollutes our rivers.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

  • Environmental StewardshipWe operate with a “Zero Waste” mindset. Our process is clean, enclosed, and designed to protect the air, soil, and water of our communities.
  • National Pride (“Made in Iraq”)We are proud to reduce reliance on imported materials. By producing rubber and fuel domestically, we keep capital within Iraq and support local supply chains.
  • Innovation & SafetyWe invest in top-tier machinery and safety protocols. Our factory is a clean, modern industrial environment where worker safety and product quality are paramount.
  • IntegrityWe build lasting partnerships with municipalities and private sectors based on transparency, reliability, and consistent output.
